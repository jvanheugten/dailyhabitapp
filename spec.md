# Daily Habit, Health, Journal, and Life tracking app

## What it does
- todo tasks and habits with reminders
- track daily habits, should be highly customizable: specific day/ optional time slots
  - Shows a daily check-in where you can fill in habit completion, short journal items
- show plots of statistics of habits over time
- track health: 
  - allow creation of specific tracking of symptoms: headaches, etc.
  - record vitals (blood pressure/sugar, ), and import from google fit
  - split by region in body
  - similar to https://www.chronicinsights.com/
    - 3d painting on body where issues arise, colored by intensity
  - detailed statstics, with correlation of symptoms with days of week, vitals, exercise, etc.
- include audio note taking
- sync/backup to cloud (google drive)
- easy export

later: 
- integrate (local) llm to ask about health and habits, and summarize journal with recommendations

## Platform
Mobile app (web-based and platform agnostic), should work well on Android
